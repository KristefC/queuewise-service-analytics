
# QueueWise — Executive Findings

## Analysis Scope

Source: NYC 311 Service Requests
Development sample: 5,000 chronologically selected
records from January 2025.

Findings below refer to the Power BI development
sample, not the separate Streamlit daily cohort.

## Key Findings

### Finding 1 — Workload concentration

Noise - Residential accounts for 2,712 of the 5,000
sampled requests (54.2%).

Its recorded median closure duration is 32.12 hours.

Interpretation:
This category dominates the observed request volume
in the development sample.

### Finding 2 — Long recorded closure durations

UNSANITARY CONDITION has 42 sampled requests and
a median closure duration of 294.41 hours.

Interpretation:
The category deserves further investigation, but
the limited sample does not establish that the
responsible agency is performing poorly.

### Finding 3 — Operational priorities differ

Some categories have high volume with relatively
short closure durations, while others have lower
volume and longer closure durations.

Interpretation:
Prioritisation should consider workload and
closure duration together rather than using
one metric in isolation.

## Recommendations

R1 — Investigate closure-time variation within
high-volume categories such as Noise - Residential.

R2 — Review request types and workflow differences
associated with long-duration categories.

R3 — Expand analysis to a full reporting period
before drawing operational conclusions.

R4 — Establish category-specific performance
benchmarks with relevant stakeholders.

## Limitations

- Chronologically selected development sample
- No confirmed root causes
- No workforce capacity or operational cost data
- No validated category-specific SLA thresholds
- Recorded closure may not equal actual issue resolution
- Historical records can change after extraction

## Business Impact

The project demonstrates decision-support
capabilities. No realised time savings, cost
reductions or operational improvements are claimed.
